# Quick Chatbot Access

Since the service cards need more complex wiring, I've added a direct chatbot button to the HomeScreen app bar.

## To Access Chatbot:
1. Login to the app
2. Look for the "💬" (chat) icon button in the top app bar
3. Tap to open the chatbot screen

The chatbot button appears next to the menu icon in the top-right corner of the home screen.
